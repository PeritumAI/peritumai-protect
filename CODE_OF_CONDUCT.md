# Code of conduct

PeritumAI Protect follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, insults,
discrimination and sharing other people's private information are not accepted in issues, pull requests or any other
project space.

Report a problem to **danilo@peritumai.com**. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments,
close issues, or block accounts that break these rules.
